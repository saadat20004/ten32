Good to know

Simple house rules

A few things that keep every event smooth. Have a question that is not covered here? Ask in your booking request and the host will reply within a few hours.

Free cancellation for 24 hours. Cancel within 24 hours of confirmation for a full refund, as long as your booking starts more than 48 hours later.

Setup and breakdown happen within your booked time, so plan for 5 to 6 hours for most events. Events of 5 hours or more can reserve extra setup time at $150 per hour for up to 5 people. Overtime is billed at 1.5x when available.

Bring your own food, refreshments and caterer. Your catering or decor team removes everything they bring in, or you can add trash removal at checkout.

BYO alcohol is welcome. No shots are served, liquor liability insurance is required, and there is zero tolerance for underage drinking.

Play amplified music from the in-house iPad or TV, or bring your own DJ. The Bose system is ready when you arrive.

Smoking and vaping are not allowed inside. Violations and other rule breaks carry a $250 fee.

The venue is on the second floor. On Sundays and after 8 PM the main elevator is off, so use the stairs or the freight elevator.

Productions with more than 19 people are priced as events. Changes to guest count or add-ons can be made within 24 hours of confirmation. A walkthrough video is available on request before you book.
